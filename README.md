# 🎵 MBC — Aaya Na Kar

> *Some songs are written to be felt, not answered.*

*A poet who never spoke chose a song instead.* 🖋️

---

## 🌙 About

**MBC** is a minimalist, emotionally driven web experience inspired by the song **“Aaya Na Kar.”**

This project isn't just a lyrics page.

It's a small digital space built around the feeling of remembering someone who perhaps shouldn't return — yet somehow keeps appearing in dreams, memories, and unfinished thoughts.

> *Words written with shaking hands by a soul that learned to let go.*

**Not a message.
Not a request.
Just a song.**

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

The design follows a simple idea:

> **Less interface. More feeling.**

Instead of overwhelming the user with buttons, menus, and unnecessary components, the website focuses on:

* Typography
* Negative space
* Music
* Lyrics
* Mood
* Visual storytelling

The goal was to make the visitor feel like they are opening a page from someone's unfinished diary.
